# Shaktiworld — Project Specification

## Purpose

Shaktiworld is a **community platform for women**. It is not a career-advice site, not a crisis hotline, and not a service provider. Career and job listings are one section among many — the platform exists to connect women with each other, with resources, and with local support networks, grounded in shared cultural identity.

## Audience

Open to **all women**, with cultural grounding in Shakti traditions spanning Hindu, Jain, Buddhist, and Sikh heritage. Membership is not limited by faith, ethnicity, or geography, but the platform's language, imagery, and community norms draw from these traditions.

## Sections

| Section | What it contains |
|---|---|
| **Services** | Curated directories and information for health, mental wellbeing, domestic violence support, and dowry-related assistance. We link to verified providers — we do not provide these services ourselves. |
| **Listening** | A peer-support space where members can share experiences and be heard. Not therapy, not counselling — human connection. |
| **Jobs** | Job listings, career resources, and professional development content. One section among several, not the product's focus. |
| **Small Business Directory** | Women-owned businesses organised by city. Visibility for members who run shops, practices, studios, and services. |
| **Events** | Community gatherings: Shakti Day, Mother's Day celebrations, workshops, cultural programmes. Past and upcoming. |
| **Education** | Learning modules, skill-building content, and training resources for personal and professional growth. |

## Access Model

- **Public pages**: Home, About, marketing content, event highlights — visible to everyone.
- **Community sections**: Services, Listening, Jobs, Small Business Directory, Education, and member-only event details all require a **verified member account** (Clerk authentication).
- No content behind a paywall at launch. Membership is free.

## Non-Goals

These are explicit boundaries the product must respect in code, copy, and design:

1. **We are not a crisis service.** The platform must never position itself as an emergency resource. It may link to crisis lines but must not replace them.
2. **We are not legal counsel.** Content about domestic violence and dowry must inform and connect — never advise on legal strategy or imply legal expertise.
3. **We are not medical providers.** Health and mental-wellbeing sections curate external resources. No diagnosis, no treatment plans, no clinical language.
4. **We are not an employment agency.** The Jobs section lists opportunities and resources. It does not guarantee placement, match candidates to employers, or act as a recruiter.
5. **Copy must never promise outcomes.** No "we will find you a job", "we will keep you safe", "we will solve your problem". The platform **connects, informs, and supports** — it does not deliver results on behalf of members.
